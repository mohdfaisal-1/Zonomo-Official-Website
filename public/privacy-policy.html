Privacy Policy

Zonomo Technologies Private Limited
Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

Zonomo is a marketplace app that connects customers with independent local service professionals ("providers") such as plumbers, electricians, cleaners and salon experts. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Account details: your name, phone number, email address, date of birth and gender. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account.

Location: with your permission, your device's precise location while you use the app, so we can show providers near you and set your service address. You can also enter an address manually. We do not collect location in the background.

Addresses and bookings: saved addresses, the services you book, booking dates and times, service addresses, prices, and the one-time codes used to start and complete a job.

Messages: chats between customers and providers about a booking.

Provider information: business name, service categories, service area, working hours, hourly rate, profile photo, and the government ID and professional licence images you upload for verification.

Subscription payments (providers only): subscriptions are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store, and purchase records are managed for us by RevenueCat. We receive the subscription status, price and transaction reference, not your card, bank or UPI details.

Technical data: basic server logs such as IP address, request time and error information, used to keep the service secure and working.

How we use your information

To create and manage your account and verify your phone number or email.

To show nearby providers, take bookings, and let customers and providers communicate about a job.

To verify providers before they are listed, and to keep the platform safe and prevent fraud or misuse.

To process provider subscription payments, respond to support requests, and meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

When we share information

Between customers and providers: when you book or accept a job, the other party sees the details needed to complete it, such as name, service address, booking details, chat messages and, where needed, phone number.

Service partners that process data on our behalf: Amazon Web Services (hosting and file storage in India), Google (Sign-In, place search and Google Play billing), Apple (App Store billing), RevenueCat (subscription records), and our SMS and email providers (one-time codes).

Legal reasons: when required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and Zonomo.

Security and retention

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit and access controls on our servers and storage.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account we remove or anonymise your personal data, except records we must keep for legal, tax or dispute-resolution purposes.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile > Delete Account in the app, or by emailing us from the email address or phone number linked to your account. You can also use https://zonomo.in/delete-account.

Deletion removes your name, phone number, email address, date of birth, gender, profile photo, saved addresses and, for providers, uploaded identity and licence documents. Past bookings stay in the other party's history without your personal details, and chat messages you already sent may remain visible to the person you sent them to.

Your choices and rights

You can view and update your details from Profile > Personal Information, turn off location access in your device settings, and request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us.

Children

Zonomo is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will show the new date at the top and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
